About

I work on distribution automation, control and telecoms. Most of what I do sits in the gap between the protection engineers and the network engineers, which turns out to be where a surprising number of interesting problems live.

These tools exist because some ideas are hard to argue about in prose. It is easier to change one variable and watch what happens to the clock. Everything here is built from public standards and published field results, with the sources stated on each page.

Engineering geek, on the evidence.
